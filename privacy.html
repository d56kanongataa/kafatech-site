Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information Kafa Tech Limited ("Kafa Tech", "we") handles when you visit kafatech.to, and how. In short: this website has no accounts, no forms, no cookies and no tracking, and we do not sell personal information.

Who we are

Kafa Tech Limited is a company registered in the Kingdom of Tonga (company no. LC9006692), based in Nuku'alofa. You can reach us about privacy at hello@kafatech.to.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the kafatech.to website, including the Fale Fonua demo at kafatech.to/fale-fonua/. Our platforms, such as Takeaway Pacific and All-Pacific Sports, run on their own websites and apps and have their own privacy policies. Please read those when you use them.

Information we collect

When you browse

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not ask you to sign up or fill in a form. Our fonts are stored on our own site, so browsing the main pages does not send requests to font or analytics companies.

Like any website, our hosting provider receives basic technical information when your device loads a page, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. The provider uses this to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix problems. We do not use it to identify you.

When you email us

If you email hello@kafatech.to, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. We use this only to reply to you and to follow up on what you asked about. We keep emails for as long as we need them for that purpose, or as required by law.

The Fale Fonua demo

The Fale Fonua demo uses sample data only. Anything you enter in the demo is saved in your own browser's local storage on your device. It is not sent to us, and you can remove it by clearing your browser's site data.

To show maps and its typeface, the demo loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap and fonts from Google Fonts. Those services receive your IP address and browser details when this happens, under their own privacy policies. If you open a video meeting link in the demo, it takes you to Jitsi Meet, a separate service. Please do not enter real personal or land information into the demo.

How we use and share information

We use the information above only to run this website and to answer your messages. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except:

  • service providers who help us run the site and our email, only as needed to provide that service;
  • where the law requires us to, or where it is needed to protect people's safety or our legal rights.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing hello@kafatech.to. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
